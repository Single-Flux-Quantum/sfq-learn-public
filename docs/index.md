## Tracks Overview

1. `sfq-logic-primitives`: Superconducting phase & flux quantization $\to$ RSFQ/ERSFQ gate primitives.
2. `clocking-biasing-power`: Clock distribution $\to$ resonant clocking $\to$ current recycling & serial biasing.
3. `eda-timing-verification`: Gate-level pipelining $\to$ STA/qSTA timing models $\to$ automated path balancing.
4. `cryogenic-interfaces-io`: $4\text{ K} \to 300\text{ K}$ links $\to$ SQUID stacks & 4JL latching drivers.
5. `cryogenic-memory`: Superconducting state storage $\to$ 0-$\pi$ SQUIDs $\to$ Josephson-CMOS hybrids.
6. `compute-neuromorphic`: Bit-parallel processors $\to$ FP ALUs $\to$ JJ-Soma spiking neural units.
7. `quantum-detector-interfaces`: Qubit control/readout drivers $\to$ SNSPD encoders $\to$ JAWS synthesizers.
