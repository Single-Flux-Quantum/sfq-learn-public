## Classical vs quantum (one line)

Classical SFQ = telegraph clicks; superconducting qubits = music-hall quantum states; SFQ may only be a pit-crew helper in a quantum fridge. Full contrast: [qubits page](superconducting-qubits-and-quantum-computing.md).

## Common misconception

**"Everything cold and Josephson is one field."** False. Junctions appear in sensors, volt standards, detectors, qubits, and classical SFQ --- with different jobs and scoreboards. That is why this folder exists.
