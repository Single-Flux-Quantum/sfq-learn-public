## Why bridge pages exist

Fundamentals teach **devices and flux**. Concepts name **cells and tools**. Between them sits a cliff: “I know what $\Phi_0$ is, but I still don’t see how a chip computes.”

Bridge pages are **longer on purpose**. They tell stories, draw waveforms, and walk tiny examples until the jump feels walkable. If a concept card feels sudden — **come back here**. Do not skip.

More wording is intentional so newcomers can follow with patience (same pedagogy spirit as [PQC Learn](https://post-quantum-cryptography-pqc.github.io/learning/)).
